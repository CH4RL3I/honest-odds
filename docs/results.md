## 4. Naive strategy check (out of sample)

**Not trading advice.** Recalibration mapping (logistic on logit(price)) is fitted on markets resolving before the split date only; bets are placed on markets whose snapshot and resolution both fall after it. A side is bought when its model-implied expected profit per 1-USD contract, after costs, exceeds the threshold. Costs: entry at price + half-spread, fee on winnings only. Fills at the snapshot price plus assumed spread; no slippage, market impact or capacity limit. Mean P&L CI: cluster bootstrap over markets.
